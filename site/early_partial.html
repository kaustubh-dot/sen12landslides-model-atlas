How 64 channels split into 16 and 48

Each replacement takes 64 learned feature channels for one date. It filters the first 16 with a 3 × 3 convolution and carries the other 48 straight to the join.

Where the split happens in the encoder

U-TAE uses a dense 3 × 3 convolution across all 64 channels at each highlighted layer. The variant runs the split and join shown above for each of the 15 dates.
